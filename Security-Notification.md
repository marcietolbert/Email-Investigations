# Security Notification

## Introduction

**Scenario:** External email received claiming to be from MetaMask Security, warning of an unrecognized login attempt and urging the recipient to verify account activity.

This report documents the analysis of a suspected phishing email leveraging cryptocurrency wallet impersonation and security-themed social engineering tactics. The message creates a sense of urgency by warning of unauthorized account access and directs the recipient to review the activity through an embedded link. The investigation examines the sender information, email authentication results, URL structure, header anomalies, and message content to determine legitimacy. The objective is to identify indicators of compromise, assess the risk of credential theft or wallet compromise, and determine whether the email represents a phishing attempt targeting cryptocurrency users.
